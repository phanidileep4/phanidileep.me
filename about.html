ABOUT ME

More than a job title.

I'm Phani Dileep — a software engineer based in the Dallas–Fort Worth area, originally from India. Technology is a big part of my life, but definitely not the whole story.

The short version

I've spent more than a decade building software and learning how large systems behave in the real world. I like engineering, but I'm equally interested in why products work, how businesses are built, how people travel, how money moves, and the small systems that make everyday life better.

This website is deliberately broader than a portfolio. Social networks never became the place where I naturally shared much. I want this to be different: a place I own, where I can write when I have something worth saying without having to fit it into somebody else's feed.

Why this site exists

Part notebook, part portfolio, part personal archive. Some posts may be deeply technical. Others might be about a road trip, a financial lesson, an idea I can't stop thinking about, or simply something I want future-me to remember.
