WORK

I build systems that need to hold up.

Senior Full Stack Engineer with 14+ years building distributed systems, cloud and data platforms, and full-stack products — increasingly using AI to improve how engineering teams design, deliver, and operate software.

The work I do

I gravitate toward complex, consequential problems: systems with large audiences, high-throughput traffic, demanding latency targets, and data models that have to keep working as their scale changes. My role often stretches from architecture and backend services through data, cloud infrastructure, delivery pipelines, testing, and the user experience.

The direction of my work is toward Staff- and Principal-caliber scope: taking ownership of ambiguous problems, connecting decisions across teams, and carrying solutions from an early idea into reliable production systems. I am also drawn to forward-deployed-style engineering — staying close to real users and operational constraints, then turning what I learn into durable platform capabilities.
